Inside Talk

Your group chat gets a place to meet.

Open a conversation's office from any message menu, or share an office link in the chat. The card shows who is there, and you can step in without leaving the conversation.

When the conversation's call starts, the office shows who is in it. Join from there, or open the full office.

What you can do

Small moments, without a meeting.

Say hi

Walk over, wave or make a coffee. Two people who wave at the same time get a high five.

Knock first

Ask “got 2 minutes?” before you call. They answer Now, In 10 minutes or Later.

Keep a desk

Claim one of 12 desks. While you are out, it shows your status and a short note for the day.

Focus together

Start a 25 or 50 minute session. Others can join, and members see a timer.

Meet someone new

Sign up for coffee roulette in a group office, and get paired for a short chat each week.

Works for everyone

Use a mouse, a phone, the keyboard or a screen reader. The people and places list does everything the map does.

Where you already work

Know who is in before you open it.

The Dashboard widget shows who is in your offices right now. Find offices in search, pick them in the Smart Picker, and open them from Team pages.

Team offices show what is shared with the Team, such as Deck boards with the cards that are due.

Privacy

You decide when you're there.

Opening an office never makes you visible; you choose Enter office. Only members of the Team, group or conversation can find and enter it.

Virtual Office keeps no history of visits or movement. Desks, notes and knocks keep only what they need, and the admin guide says when each is removed.

What you need

Nextcloud 35. No extra server. Group offices work without other apps.

Talk adds conversation offices and calls (tested with Talk 25). Teams adds Team offices, Deck adds due cards, and Client Push makes movement instant.
